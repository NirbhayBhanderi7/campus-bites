Redefining Campus
Dining.

Campus Bites was born from a simple frustration: long lines and cold food. Our mission is to seamlessly connect students with fresh, delicious meals across campus, minimizing wait times and maximizing study (or chill) time. We envision a campus where food access is instant, equitable, and completely stress-free.

How It Works

Three simple steps to your next meal.

1. Order

Browse menus from all campus canteens and customize your meal directly in the app.

2. Track

Get real-time updates on your order status. Know exactly when it's being prepared.

3. Pickup

Skip the line. Head straight to the pickup counter when your meal is ready.
